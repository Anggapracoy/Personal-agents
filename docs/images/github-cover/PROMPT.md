## Generation prompt

Create a polished GitHub project cover image in exact 16:9 landscape ratio, ideally 3840x2160. Pure white background. At the top, centered large black clean bold sans-serif typography with exactly this headline: "Dash - your proactive assistant". Generous white space and immaculate alignment. Below the headline, place exactly THREE upright iPhone app screenshots side by side at equal scale, with subtly rounded corners, restrained dark thin device frames and very soft realistic shadows. All three phones fully visible with comfortable white margins. Front-on, no perspective, no overlapping phones. Use the three provided actual iPhone simulator screenshots as the screen content: image 1 on the LEFT is the home/feed; image 2 in the CENTER is the proactive headphone return chat; image 3 on the RIGHT is the Montreal trip reply with structured stats and itinerary cards AND the iPhone keyboard open. Preserve each source screenshot's real UI faithfully, including the colorful mascot avatars, text, buttons, exact structured trip cards, and keyboard. Do not invent substitute UI, extra cards, decorative charts, or new screen text. The Montreal phone must show ONLY the trip-related reply/cards already in its screenshot, not contacts, email drafts, tables or unrelated demo content. Do not add slogans, captions, badges, gradients, decorative objects, logos, watermarks or any other text outside the specified headline and the screenshots. Premium, minimalist product presentation; black headline on white with the app UI providing the only color.
